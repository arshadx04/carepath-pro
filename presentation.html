Clinical Algorithm

5-Step Intelligent Triage & Urgency Stratification

Standardized protocol balancing clinical sensitivity with specificity.

5-Step Clinical Assessment Flow

Intake Methodology

  • Step 1: Patient Demographics — Age group (Infant, Child, Adult, Senior), Biological sex, Pregnancy status, Comorbidities (Hypertension, Diabetes, Asthma, Smoking).
  • Step 2: Anatomical Symptom Selection — Searchable index with body-region filters.
  • Step 3: Timeline & Trajectory — Sudden onset (minutes) vs gradual (days); Worsening vs steady.
  • Step 4: Severity Metrics — Visual Analog Scale (VAS 0–10) & daily disruption scale.
  • Step 5: Mandatory Red-Flag Screener — Critical life-safety check.
4-Tier Urgency Classification

Action Thresholds

  • LEVEL 1: EMERGENCY (Immediate ER / 911)
    Triggered by active red-flags, chest pressure, stroke FAST signs, or pain >= 9.
  • LEVEL 2: URGENT CARE (Within 12–24 Hours)
    Spreading infections, high fever, severe flares, vulnerable demographics.
  • LEVEL 3: PRIMARY CARE (Routine Outpatient)
    Subacute or chronic conditions (Tension headache, GERD, Osteoarthritis, GAD).
  • LEVEL 4: SUPPORTIVE CARE (Home Monitoring)
    Mild stable symptoms (pain <= 3, improving trajectory).
